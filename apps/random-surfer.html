Follow the links, rank the pages

A random surfer clicks a random link on each page, and now and then jumps to a random page instead. The fraction of time spent on each page is its PageRank: the steady state of a Markov chain, found by the power method.

Bigger circle: higher PageRank the surfer’s last move

Click one page and then another to add or remove a link between them. Click the same page twice to cancel.
