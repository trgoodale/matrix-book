Turn the joints, place the hand

Each link of a robot arm is a rotation followed by a translation, which one 3 × 3 matrix in homogeneous coordinates can hold. Multiply the matrices for the two links and the last column tells you where the hand is. Or move the target (drag it, or click where it should go) and let Newton’s method find the joint angles.
