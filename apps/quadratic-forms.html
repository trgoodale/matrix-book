The shape of xᵀAx

For a symmetric matrix A, the quadratic form q(x) = xᵀAx gives a number at every point of the plane. Its contours are ellipses, hyperbolas or straight lines, and their axes are the eigenvectors of A.

q > 0 q < 0 q = 1 q = −1 eigenvectors (principal axes)
