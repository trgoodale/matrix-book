Split a network, find who matters

The graph Laplacian L = D − A has one zero eigenvalue for each piece of the network. The eigenvector of the next eigenvalue, the Fiedler vector, splits the network into two groups by its signs; the leading eigenvector of A measures how central each node is.

Click one node and then another to add or remove the edge between them. Click the same node twice to cancel.
