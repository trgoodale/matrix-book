Load the bridge, find the forces

A pin-jointed truss carries loads by pulling and pushing along its members. The app writes the equilibrium of every joint as one linear system and solves it. Click a joint to move the load there; click a member to remove it, and see whether the structure still stands.

tension (pulled) compression (pushed) deflected shape (exaggerated)

Thicker members carry larger forces. Triangles are pinned supports; circles under a triangle are rollers, which can only push up. With the picture focused, the left and right arrow keys move the load.
